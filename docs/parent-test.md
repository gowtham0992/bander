# Parent comprehension test

Status: **planned, not yet performed**.

Use the real product with fictional Calendar data and the invited family contact. Do not teach internal vocabulary or provide a magic sentence. Ask the participant to think aloud; help only if they are stuck.

## Tasks

1. Ask what is on the Calendar tomorrow.
2. Ask to move one eligible appointment and let Gil know.
3. Before approving, explain in their own words both things Bander says will happen.
4. Approve, then explain how they know the Calendar changed and the update was sent.
5. Prepare another request and choose **Not now**. Explain what happened.
6. Ask for an unsupported grocery order. Identify the safe next step.
7. Disconnect Gil. Explain what Bander can and cannot do afterward.

## Success criteria

- The participant distinguishes OpenClaw conversation from Bander approval and outcome messages.
- They understand schedule reading needs no approval because it changes nothing.
- They identify both effects and the complete before/after time before approving.
- They understand **Do exactly this** applies once and **Not now** changes nothing.
- They do not believe Telegram acceptance proves Gil read the update.
- They understand disconnecting Gil prevents future family delivery without changing owner control.
- Every unsupported, declined, or failed path leaves them with a useful next step.

## Post-test questions

- What did OpenClaw do? What did Bander do?
- At what moment did anything gain permission to change?
- What exactly did your tap allow?
- What would you expect if the Calendar changed before your tap?
- What does “sent to Gil” prove—and what does it not prove?
- Was any wording confusing, technical, or too small to scan?
